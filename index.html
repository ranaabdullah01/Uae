UAE GOLDEN VISA

Your pathway to 10‑year renewable residency in the UAE

What is the Golden Visa?

The UAE Golden Visa is a long-term residence visa valid for 10 years, renewable, and without the need for a sponsor. It offers investors, entrepreneurs, and talented individuals the opportunity to live, work, and study in the UAE.

Property Investment Requirements

  • Minimum investment of AED 2,000,000 in property
  • Property can be off-plan or ready
  • Property must be owned outright (not mortgaged)
  • Valid for the investor, spouse, and children

Golden Visa Benefits

10‑Year Residency

Renewable visa for 10 years without a sponsor

Family Inclusion

Includes spouse, children, and parents

Business Opportunities

Easier to start and manage businesses in UAE

Travel Flexibility

Unrestricted travel in and out of UAE

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum investment for Golden Visa? +
The minimum investment is AED 2,000,000 in property. This can be a single property or multiple properties totaling AED 2M+.
Can I apply with an off-plan property? +
Yes, off-plan properties are eligible if they are registered with the Dubai Land Department and meet the minimum value requirement.
How long does the Golden Visa last? +
The Golden Visa is valid for 10 years and is renewable.
Can I include my family? +
Yes, you can include your spouse, children, and even parents under your Golden Visa.
What if I sell the property? +
You must maintain the AED 2M+ investment. Selling the property may affect your visa status.
